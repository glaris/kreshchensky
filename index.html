Evgeny Kreshchensky

Fine Artist & Scenographer

Evgeny Kreshchensky is a Belgrade-based Fine Artist and Scenographer. His work explores the intersection of theatrical space and graphic form, focusing on the raw aesthetics of nature and structural symbolism. Selected works from the “THE HEAD GARDEN: Freedom Vibes” series are currently on view at the Avala Tower, Belgrade (covered by Blic and LookerWeekly). You can also read the recent interview with Evgeny in FashionMag42.
